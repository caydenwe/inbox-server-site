Privacy policy

inbox-server · Last updated 12 October 2026

Who uses it

inbox-server is used only by its owner, with their own Google accounts. It has no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads emails in the owner's Gmail accounts: senders, subjects, dates, labels and message text. At first it has read-only access. If the owner later turns on inbox tidying, it can also add or remove labels, archive emails and mark them read. It never sends, forwards or permanently deletes email.

How the data is used

Only to sort the owner's email, track their to-dos, and suggest inbox changes for the owner to approve.

Where it's processed and stored

On the owner's own computers. Email text is analysed by AI models running on those computers; it is not sent to any cloud AI service. A copy of the message text is kept for up to 30 days after an email has been dealt with, then deleted, and straight away if the owner deletes the email. The owner can keep the text of particular emails longer, as examples for testing the app; these stay on the owner's own computers. Backup copies on those computers are kept for up to 30 days. Summaries and notifications are sent to the owner's private Discord server.

Sharing

Data from Google accounts is not sold, shared, or used for advertising, and isn't used to train AI models. It isn't transferred to anyone except as described above.

Google API Services

inbox-server's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. Stored data is deleted when the app's database is deleted.
